I am a PostDoc at the Institute for theoretical physics at Heidelberg University in the group of Prof. Tilman Plehn.
My research focuses on bridging particle physics and machine learning. Mainly, investigating the effect of uncertainties in machine learning architectures and how they could be used in particle physics analyses. Starting from global SMEFT and EDM analyses and moving further to developing fast and precise amplitude surrogates, my interests are 
- Surrogate networks
- Uncertainty estimation using ML
- Global Analyses
- Statistics
